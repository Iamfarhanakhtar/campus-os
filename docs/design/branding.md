# CampusOS Branding Guidelines

## Logo & Identity
The CampusOS identity combines a bold square monogram ("C") with a vibrant purple-to-indigo gradient (`#7C5CFC` to `#5824E3`).

- **Monogram Icon**: `/design/logo/logo.svg`
- **Favicon**: `/design/favicon/favicon.svg`

## Aesthetic Principles
- **No Heavy Gradients**: Avoid loud rainbow gradients. Restrain accents to subtle highlights and soft glowing backdrops.
- **Precision Over Decoration**: Every visual element serves data clarity.
- **Glassmorphism**: Use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` for overlays, headers, and floating dialogs.
