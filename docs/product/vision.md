# CampusOS — Product Vision & Strategic Mission

## Core Mission
CampusOS is an AI-powered College Operating System designed to replace fragmented student tools (spreadsheets, paper planners, disconnected calendar apps, Notion templates) with a single, unified, high-performance workspace.

## Target Audience
- University and college students pursuing rigorous academic tracks.
- Engineering, CS, Medical, Business, and Law students managing heavy course loads.
- Students seeking proactive AI assistance for exam prep, attendance safeguards, and time allocation.

## Design Inspiration
Inspired by premium productivity applications:
- **Linear**: Keyboard-first navigation, sleek dark theme, high density information presentation.
- **Raycast**: Quick command palette (`⌘K`), instant modal search, launcher speed.
- **Arc Browser**: Smooth sidebar hierarchy, workspace grouping, ambient aesthetics.
- **Apple & Vercel**: Minimalist typography, subtle glassmorphism, crisp borders.

## Value Proposition
1. **Attendance Assurance**: Real-time margin calculators preventing accidental exam disqualifications.
2. **AI Study Acceleration**: Automated flashcard generation, lecture summarization, and focus zone optimization.
3. **Unified Academic Trajectory**: Single-screen clarity across course grades, projects, deadlines, and career goals.
